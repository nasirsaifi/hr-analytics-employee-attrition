n Project Overview
An end-to-end HR Analytics project analyzing employee attrition, workforce trends, salary, satisfaction and performance
to identify high-risk employee groups and support better HR decisions.
n Business Problem
Why is employee attrition happening, and which employee groups are at higher risk?
The analysis examines overtime, job satisfaction, work-life balance, tenure, salary, performance, departments and job
roles to identify patterns related to employee attrition.

n Key Findings
• 20.8% overall attrition was observed.
• Employees working overtime show higher attrition.
• Low job satisfaction is associated with higher attrition.
• Employees with 0–2 years of tenure represent a higher-risk group.
• Low work-life balance is associated with higher attrition.
• Some job roles and departments show higher attrition than others.
These are patterns observed in the dataset and should not be treated as proof of direct causation.
n Business Decisions
• Review overtime and workload in high-attrition groups.
• Create targeted retention programs for early-tenure employees.
• Improve job satisfaction and work-life balance initiatives.
• Investigate departments and job roles with consistently high attrition.
• Review compensation and employee benefits for higher-risk groups.
• Monitor attrition regularly through the Power BI dashboard.
